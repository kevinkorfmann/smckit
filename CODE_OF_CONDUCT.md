# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Be respectful, assume good intent while responding to impact, keep scientific
criticism specific and evidence-based, and do not disclose private information.
Unacceptable behavior may be removed and contributors may be temporarily or
permanently excluded.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the security contact
documented in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
